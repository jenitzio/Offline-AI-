🌊 About Ocean Chat

Ocean Chat is a tiny AI assistant that runs entirely in your browser. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

What happens when you click "Load model"?

  • Your browser downloads SmolLM2 135M Instruct from Hugging Face — about 110 MB.
  • The model is cached locally (in your browser's storage), so it only downloads once.
  • After loading, you can disconnect from the internet — chats run offline on your device.
  • Nothing you type ever leaves your computer.
